Why me.

I have never held the title Product Manager. I started as a front-end developer, and today I am the product owner and builder of my own products and of the tools a ten-team program plans from.

The gap, plainly

I have not worked at a product-led software company of Linear’s kind, and my titles have been Scrum Master, Agile Coach and Release Train Engineer.

My product work is on my own products and on tools inside a program. I have not owned a feature with a sales team waiting on the message.

What I have done is the work in the posting’s four bullets at a smaller size: ask the users, write it down, build it, and explain it to the people who have to adopt it. I have also lived the problem on this site from the other side, running multi-team programs in Jira.

Linear’s hiring page says it values slope over credentials. This site is my evidence of slope: one finding, taken from reading to a working page, in a day.
